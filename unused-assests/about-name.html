Why the Name RAM Digital Media?

RAM Digital Media is more than just a business name — it carries a personal story and meaning.

I am the only child of my father, Rajendra Amarchand Meher, and taking his name forward has always been something very close to my heart. The name RAM comes from his initials, and choosing this name was my way of honoring his identity while building something meaningful for the future.

By combining RAM with Digital Media, I wanted to create something that represents both generations — the values and legacy of the past, along with the innovation and creativity of the new digital generation.

For me, RAM Digital Media is not just a company. It is a journey of combining legacy, passion, and creativity to build something meaningful in today’s digital world.
